AI Agent Hub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of information in the currently available AI Agent Hub web application. Update this policy whenever the application's actual data practices change.

1. Information you enter

You may enter project names, social account identifiers, content drafts, manually recorded performance or earnings information, and server connection settings. Do not enter social-media passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information.

2. Where information is stored

In the current web version, application records are designed to be stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. If you configure the optional AI Agent Hub Cloudflare Worker connection, information sent to that endpoint is processed by that service to respond to your request. Review and configure your Worker and its retention practices before using it with personal information.

3. Social platform access

Adding an account record does not itself authorize access to TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube. OAuth authorization and live platform integrations are not active unless separately implemented, approved, and clearly presented in the app. When a platform integration is added, this policy must be updated to identify the data requested, purpose, storage, retention, and deletion process.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers needed to operate configured services, or disclosed where required by law. Do not use the app for information you are not authorized to share.

5. Retention and deletion

Records stored in browser local storage remain on that browser/device until you clear the app's site data or the application provides a deletion control. Data sent to a configured third-party Worker may be subject to that provider's configuration and logs. Check those settings separately.

6. Security

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device secure, do not share bearer tokens or API secrets, and rotate any credential you believe has been exposed.

7. Children and changes

AI Agent Hub is a business/productivity tool and is not intended for children to use independently. This policy may be updated when functionality or data practices change.
